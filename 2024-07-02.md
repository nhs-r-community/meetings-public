**Conference update**

[Sponsorship]{.underline}

organisation.

event. Jumping Rivers provided a contact at the Royal Statisical Society
to see if they would like to have the final stand and provide

Anastasiia will chase NHSE again for an update on possible sponsorship.

[Conference 2025]{.underline}

We will also need to start to think about what we want to do next year
and whether we wish to hold another conference in the Birmingham area or
go to another region, taking account of accessibility and carbon
footprint.  We agreed to wait until after HACA.

We can ask the question about location in the RPySOC 24 post-conference
feedback (but bear in mind that this is people who attended!).  We will
also ask virtual attendees what the barriers (if any) to attending were
and whether certain locations would have made them want to attend in
person.

[Surplus funds]{.underline}

Due to careful spending, there will be some surplus budget for RPySOC
24.  We agreed that a small spend on some directional floor stickers at
the venue would be a good idea and an effective use of funds.  It is
important that all attending feel welcome, particularly if they are
travelling to and attending the conference alone.

We would also like to offer help with travel and accommodation for
people who want to attend but are unable to obtain funds from their
employer.  We will need to see some proof that the travel/accommodation
has been declined, such as an email from their manager.  We will also
need to cap the amount for each person.  It will be particularly
important for those presenting at the conference.  We would book the
travel and accommodation and be subject to our procedures at NHS ML.

[Abstracts]{.underline}

All are reminded that the deadline for receipt of abstracts is 26 July
and so far just three have been received. 

[NHS-R accounts]{.underline}

Zoë is looking at using SubStack to take all email addresses from the
current Wordpress site and ask if people would like to sign up to a
newsletter;  this should be an effective way to promote events and NHS-R
generally.

The NHS-R Posit cloud licence is due for renewal.  However, as this is
not being used as extensively as previously, it was agreed that it would
be better to not renew and save circa several thousands of pounds.
