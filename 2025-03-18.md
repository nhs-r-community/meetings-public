## Events

### RPYSOC 2025

It was queried whether a conference should be hosted this year,
regardless of funding, due to the recent announcements relating to NHS
England and the transformation of the NHS. It was argued that the
requirement for a conference has increased as a means to show the
analytics community is not in decline, facilitate networking
opportunities and provide a crucial safe space. That being said, it was
worth noting that as a result of reduced funding and roles within the
NHS, not all community members may be able to afford travel and/or
accommodation to attend. An online conference may cause difficulties for
any community members that do not own/have access to appropriate
technology to be able to attend.

There is no current plan to rebrand the conference whether it be online
or hybrid (if funding becomes available).

## Feedback and KPIs

### NHS-R Community Questionnaire 2025

In order to gather important efficiency and utilisation data to aid the
request for funding and sponsorship, a questionnaire is to be
distributed to community members.

Committee members were provided with a link to preview the
questionnaire.

An initial list of Fellows has been drafted consisting of members that
automatically qualify for the title such as committee members, community
team members, etc.

An application form is to be created to allow NHS-R Community members to
apply for the honorary title of Fellow.

The application criteria is as follows: Members of the NHS-R Community
Committee who have attended 3 or more committee meetings within the past
12 months will be awarded Fellow status. Similarly, community members
who are on the Conference organisation committee, and all members of the
NHS-R Community Core Team will automatically qualify for Fellowships.

Fellowships will also be awarded to nominees who can evidence 3 or more
of the following within the past 12 months: • Blog submission and
publication on the NHS-R Community website • Pull request of reasonable
size submitted to one of the NHS-R Community GitHub repositories •
Leading (or co-piloting) a training session • Delivering a webinar •
Delivering a workshop • Submitting a usable non-code contribution, for
example NHS-R artwork • Representation of the NHS-R Community at
external events • Any other contribution deemed ‘significant’ by the
core team.

The Fellow title will only be valid for 3 years. If a community member
would like to reinstate their Fellow title, they will be required to
submit a new application.

The NHS-R Community has a limited number of badges available for
Fellows. The badges will be distributed via postal service thus, some
personal data will need to be collected and momentarily stored in
compliance with IG and GDPR regulations. It was queried whether a list
of Fellows and their contributions could be added to the NHS-R Way Book
and/or NHS-R Community website, to make the information easily
accessible to community members.

It might be beneficial to provide prompts, via email, to community
members that are close to meeting the minimum requirement to apply for
the honorary title of Fellow. It was acknowledged that this could become
administratively burdensome for the core team to manage.

It was agreed that fellowship applications that do not clearly comply
with the aforementioned requirements, will be reviewed and approved or
rejected by a Fellowship Review Panel. The Fellowship Review Panel will
consist of the NHS-R Community Core Team with the addition of at least
one member of the NHS-R Community Committee as an impartial member. A
list of the review panel members will be published to the NHS-R Way Book
and/or NHS-R Community website.

### Should the NHS-R and Python Communities merge?

It was queried if it would be beneficial to merge the NHS-R and Python
Communities to reduce the risk of either (or both) loosing viability as
a result of reduced funding and/or support. Concerns were raised that by
doing so the communities may become more diluted, causing members to
seek alternate more specialised communities and resources. It was agreed
that the cooperation and partnership between the communities will
continue however, there are no plans to join them at this time. This is
not to say that it will not be discussed in 6 to 12 months when the
impact of the transformation has become more quantifiable.
