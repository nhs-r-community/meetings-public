## Fellowship

### Fellows

It was confirmed that the NHS-R Community website has a live Fellowships page which lists 
current NHS-R Community Fellows. 
It was noted that an email has been distributed to current NHS-R Community Committee members 
requesting permission to post their name and organisation on the website for three years. 
Additional information regarding who can apply, what being a Fellow entails, and the minimum 
requirements to achieve the honorary title can be found on the NHS-R Community Way Book.

It was confirmed that, although in previous years there have been multiple titles available, 
you can now only hold the title of Fellow. 

Fellow pin badges are available however, there is only a very limited number. 

Regular social media posts are to be scheduled to advertise the fellowship. 
It was advised that a reference to the fellowship should be included when promoting any 
activity that could contribute towards achieving the title such as blogs, webinars, workshops, etc. 
In addition, it may be beneficial to maintain records of community members’ contributions during 
the year and, for those that are one contribution away from qualifying, a brief email be provided 
advising of the fellowship. This would involve significant administrative effort to manage. 
It was suggested that a badge be created for use on Slack, not dissimilar to the tick on X, 
which when clicked on would redirect the user to the NHS-R Community Way Book or website.

There are badge designs available that could be resized and provided to Fellows for 
inclusion on their email signatures.

Adrian Pratt volunteered to be a member of the adjudication panel.
